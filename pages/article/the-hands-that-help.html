The Hands That Help


by Sarah Sanchez



We are the hands that help.

We help those in need and those who need a friend.

We are the hands that work in secret, to help the community.

We are the hands that help make the community feel safe, not in fear and terror.

We are the hands that bring community together during rough times because no one gets left out.

We are the hands that help defend our neighbor during times of deportation.

We are the hands that build a community from nothing.

We are the hands that help the people rise up from oppression.

We are the hands that love each other and support each other.

We are the hands that help that have nothing to lose but our chains.

Sarah Sanchez is a freshman at Democracy Prep High School and active member of the Liberation Program at Brotherhood Sister Sol with an affinity for anime, poetry, and community organizing.
