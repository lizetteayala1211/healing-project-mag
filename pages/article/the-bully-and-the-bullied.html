The Bully and the Bullied


by Urhiiness Hill



bullying.

the one thing in life everyone has in common

either you’re the bully, or the bullied.

as a darkskin, plus-size girl

who had just did her first big chop,

which do you think I was?

i got into my dream school

a performing arts school in the middle of times square

i wanted to be on broadway

i wanted to shine

but now i try to avoid midtown as much as possible because the memories of the big city lights bring back terror in my eyes.

first it was jokes

then the looks

then the whispers that snuck into silence

my friends avoided me

to protect their own names.

then it got louder

then it got mean

and quickly it got dangerous.

i wanted to stand up for myself

because nobody else would

not the teachers

the same ones who say

“we don’t tolerate bullying”

right before they turn a blind eye.

she was bigger, taller, but I was stronger in my heart.

still I turned to violence

and that was my mistake.

because violence never saves you

it just gives the system another reason to punish you.

after all the torment

all the laughter

all the names

all the hate

i was the one who got the superintendent suspension?

how is that fair?

because I stopped being a punk?

because the school failed to protect me?

because my mental health broke

under the weight of every lunch period

where I sat alone, pretending I didn't hear them?

is this what high school is supposed to feel like?

helpless.

small.

unseen.

if someone had spoken up,

if someone had said,

“don’t talk about her hair”

or “don’t laugh at her clothes”

if someone had just said stop.

maybe I’d still be there

walking through times square

dreaming about the stage lights instead of detention lights

thinking about my lines instead of my record.

but now i go to school in the upper west side

metal detectors at the door

police in disguise

and i don’t dream about broadway anymore.

i dream about becoming a teacher

the kind of teacher who notices the quiet girls who resemble me

and says “you belong here”
