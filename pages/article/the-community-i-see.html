The Community I See


by Aminata Konate



Hey there,
What is your community like?
Is it as welcoming as it is whole?
Is it as safe as it is open?
Is it as peaceful as it is helpful?

Cuz if not,
Why do you call it what it isn’t?
It’s the same reason why it’s uncomfortable to be in a room full of judging eyes
The same reason why a community is a place we uphold, not let fall

That we don’t break away from at the smallest issue
And call it pointless when things go to hell

We don’t let each other fall if one of us is suffering
We don’t let them go through it alone
We don’t watch them crumble while we laugh and enjoy
While they watch from afar

Being a community isn’t only about unity
It’s about loving and caring for those around us
Even if they ain’t one of us
Even if they are new
We accept them more than we accept what we haven’t done

We can be that future
But for that to happen we need to keep our people together
To not let small things take us down
Or we will never lose those chains that we swore we would
The legacy we swore to protect
The future we haven’t perfected

Cus we will fight like our ancestors Harriet Tubman and Madam CJ
Cus that’s all we’ve ever done
Fight like hell
