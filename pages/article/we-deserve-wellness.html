We Deserve Wellness


by members of The Liberation Program at Brotherhood Sister Sol



Reflections provided by members of The Liberation Program at Brotherhood Sister Sol. This group is calling on the New York City Department of Education (DOE) and elected officials to invest in wellness centers for every school campus in NYC, prioritizing the schools with the least amount of mental health support first. A wellness center is a dedicated space in a school building solely for the enrichment of young people’s spirit, mental health, and overall well-being.
